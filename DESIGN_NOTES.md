# Century Academy · design notes

Service website for online English tuition. The first action is to find a course or arrange a free trial. Baseline screenshots in `qa/before` cover 360, 768 and 1280 px.

- Keep the green, ivory and gold palette, serif display type, Roman numerals, classical art and existing page hierarchy.
- At 360 px the artwork begins below the first screen. At 768 px a single “A” occupies a headline line. Tighten the hero and allow the heading to wrap naturally. Preserve the painting’s central figures and keep its credit clear of the label.
- Make fees and the trial reachable from every page. Give uncertain families a short finder without weakening direct access to the six guides.
- Replace undersized captions, labels and navigation with readable text. Keep the sticky navigation and trial bar clear of focused controls.
- Keep the supplied section order. Use the painting and the experience figure as visual anchors, with restrained motion and a static reduced-motion presentation.
- Use clearly labelled AED demonstration rates for the GCC preview. Confirmed contact and incentive content comes from the brief.
