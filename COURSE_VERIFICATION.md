## Changes from the prior guides

- **0500** now gives task-level marks, word counts, formats, entry exclusions and the optional speaking endorsement in an expandable guide. The finder repeats the two requested entry exclusions with the exam-centre prompt. See pp. 13–18, 29–30 and 35.
- **0475** now states the 2028 coursework marks and approximate word counts separately and includes the drama requirement. The 2027 and 2028 routes remain separate. See the 2028–2030 syllabus pp. 9 and 24–25.
- **2010** eligibility wording follows Cambridge’s notice. The PDFs direct centres to the syllabus page on p. 1. The notice lists administrative zones 3, 4 and 5 for 2027 and zone 4 only from 2028. The guide asks families to confirm eligibility with their exam centre and makes no GCC-wide entry claim. [Cambridge 2010 syllabus page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-o-level-literature-in-english-2010/)
- **9695** P4 now specifies one pre-1900 and one post-1900 set text, with one poetry and one prose response. The choice for each text is a general essay or a question on the printed passage or poem. See pp. 45–46.
- The durations, component totals and qualification weightings in the existing tables agreed with the official assessment overviews. The 21 supported year and route combinations pass the local data check.
